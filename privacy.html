Privacy

What this site knows about you.

Almost nothing. Here is exactly what leaves your browser, what stays on your device, and what I can see.

No analytics, no cookies, no accounts

This site runs no analytics and sets no cookies. There is no tracking pixel, no advertising tag, and no third-party script of any kind. Nothing you do here is measured, profiled, or sold, and there is no account to create.

The one thing stored on your device is your light or dark theme preference, kept in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers it on your next visit. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

The contact form does not send data here

The form on the contact page has no server behind it. When you submit it, your browser opens your own email client with the message pre-filled. Nothing is transmitted to this site, and nothing is stored on it. You are the one who decides whether to press send, and the message goes straight to my inbox like any other email.

If you email me, whether through that form or directly, I keep the message in my inbox so I can reply. I do not add you to a mailing list.

One request leaves your browser

The home page shows what I am listening to. To do that, your browser asks Last.fm for my most recent track. That request goes from you to Last.fm directly, so their servers can see your IP address, as they would for any site you visit. It carries no information about you from me, and if you block it the rest of the page works normally. Last.fm handles that request under their own privacy policy.

Everything else on the page, including all fonts and images, is served from this domain.

Hosting

The site is static and hosted on Netlify, which keeps standard server logs of requests made to it. Those logs are Netlify’s, handled under their privacy policy. I do not receive them and cannot look you up in them.

Your rights, and how to use them

Because the site collects nothing, there is generally nothing of yours for me to hand over or delete. The exception is email you have sent me. Ask and I will delete it, and tell you what I held.

Questions about any of this go to hello@miebaka.design.
