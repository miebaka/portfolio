What this site knows about you.

Almost nothing. Here is exactly what leaves your browser, what stays on your device, and what I can see.

No analytics, no cookies, no accounts

This site runs no analytics and sets no cookies. There is no tracking pixel, no advertising tag, and no third-party script of any kind. Nothing you do here is measured, profiled, or sold, and there is no account to create.

The one thing stored on your device is your light or dark theme preference, kept in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers it on your next visit. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Contact messages

When you submit the contact form, it sends your name, email address, optional area of interest and message so I can read and reply to your enquiry. Resend processes the message to deliver it to my inbox. Resend handles that information under its privacy policy.

The form also sends a page-start time and an empty anti-spam field. These help reject automated submissions. To limit repeated attempts, the server converts your network address into a keyed, non-reversible identifier and keeps attempt times in a rolling 15-minute window. The contact service records request identifiers, response status, timing and provider result class for operational logging; it does not put your name, email address or message in those logs.

Accepted messages remain in my inbox so I can reply and keep the relevant correspondence. I do not add you to a mailing list or sell your details. I do not promise a fixed deletion period that the service does not currently enforce.

External links

The home page shows my latest listening status. The portfolio server asks Last.fm for the latest track; your browser does not receive the Last.fm API key or contact Last.fm unless you choose to open the track link. Last.fm handles that visit under its own privacy policy.

All fonts and images are served from this domain.

Hosting

The site is static and hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel receives request information such as IP address, device details and basic network logs when it serves the site. Vercel handles that information under its privacy notice. I do not run separate analytics or tracking on top of that hosting.

Your rights, and how to use them

You can ask what contact information I hold or ask me to delete a message you sent. I will handle that request through the email address below, subject to any information I must keep for legal or security reasons.
