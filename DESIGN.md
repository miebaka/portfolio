# Miebaka Joshua — Editorial Swiss system

The site uses an editorial reading voice inside a Swiss structural system: strict roles, restrained colour, long measures, asymmetrical grids and generous intervals. It preserves the original repository palette and typography.

## Typography

| Role | Face | Range | Leading | Use |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Poster | TeX Gyre Heros | 56–96px | 1.031 | Name and rare signature statements |
| Hero | TeX Gyre Heros | 36–56px | 1.062 | Page openings |
| H1 | TeX Gyre Heros | 32–48px | 1.08 | Primary headings |
| H2 | TeX Gyre Heros | 26–36px | 1.08 | Section statements |
| H3 | TeX Gyre Heros | 22–28px | 1.08 | Subheads and work titles |
| Body | Libre Baskerville | 16–18px | 1.72 | Reading copy |
| Label | Josefin Sans Semibold | 12px | 1.35 | Navigation, metadata and controls; always uppercase |

Display tracking tightens as size increases. Reading measures stop at 34em; captions stop at 21em. Labels use 0.18em tracking.

## Space and layout

The base rhythm is 8px. Page gutters scale from 28px to 120px. Editorial column gaps scale from 48px to 128px; list rows from 36px to 64px; major sections from 96px to 192px. Six-column work catalogues step down to three columns at 1100px and two at 640px.

## Colour

- Paper `#f4f1ea`
- Ink `#16140f`
- Clay `#c2531c`
- Accessible clay text `#b24c1a`

The boomslang's green exists only during page transitions, so it reads as an authored interruption rather than a fourth interface colour.

## Motion

Motion is limited to authored signatures: the dimensional logo, selected-work fill, journey ticker, particle signature and boomslang page transition. Every continuous scene pauses outside its useful lifecycle; reduced-motion users receive static fallbacks and direct navigation.
