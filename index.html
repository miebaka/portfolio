ABOUT

Hi, I’m Miebaka. I design brand identities, campaigns and graphic systems. I care as much about how they survive production as how they look in the presentation.

Over the last seven years, I’ve helped teams turn unclear ideas into useful, memorable systems. I like sharp thinking, expressive typography, generous collaborators and work that still makes sense after the handoff.

My practice moves between strategy, art direction and production for technology, finance, culture and self-initiated projects. This site is a record of the decisions, experiments and finished things along the way.

How I work

  1. Clarify

    Find the decision underneath the brief and define what the work must do.

  2. Decide

    Choose a visual and verbal direction strong enough to guide the whole system.

  3. Build

    Develop the identity across the moments where people will actually meet it.

  4. Hand over

    Deliver the tools, rules and files a team needs to keep the work intact.
