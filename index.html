How I work

A clear route through a complicated problem.

The method is not a rigid formula. It is a sequence that keeps ideas, production and people moving in the same direction.

  1. 01

    Question

    Find the real problem beneath the first version of the brief.

  2. 02

    Decide

    Choose one governing idea that can carry the next hundred choices.

  3. 03

    Design

    Translate the decision into a recognisable visual and verbal system.

  4. 04

    Test

    Put the system through real formats, constraints, handoffs and production.

  5. 05

    Equip

    Leave the team with tools and rules they can keep using without me.
