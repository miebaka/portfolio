Hi, I’m Miebaka. I design for brands across technology, finance and culture.

I started during NYSC in 2017. My work now spans visual identities, campaign graphics, presentations and printed materials.

I work through strategy, art direction and production, staying with a project through the artwork, the handoff and the printer. The cases here show the design choices and the resulting applications.
