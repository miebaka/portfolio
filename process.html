How a project works.

The goal is not just a convincing presentation. It is a direction people can understand, use and maintain.

01: Discover

We identify the problem, audience, constraints and the decision the brief needs. Useful inputs include a project summary, decision-makers, budget range, launch window and examples of what is not working.

Rivo Studio: from positioning problem to design decision

02: Decide

We explore viable creative directions, make trade-offs visible and agree on one clear direction before multiplying the work into more formats.

TEDx RSU: the Pause creative decision

03: Build the system

We design the identity, campaign or communication system across the touchpoints the project needs, testing clarity and production constraints as it develops.

Rivo Studio: wordmark, palette and guidelines

04: Handoff

We deliver approved assets, rules and working files for practical use. Revision rounds, responsibilities and production needs are agreed before work begins.

ChopBox: proposed applications and production limits

Scope and timeline

Timeline and fees depend on the agreed scope. Production, photography, development and other specialist services are scoped separately where needed. This page is not a fixed-price or delivery-time promise.
