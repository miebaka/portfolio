I ask until the idea is clear. Then I make it useful.

I’m Miebaka, a brand and visual designer in Lagos. I want to know what the headline means before choosing its typeface.

I started during NYSC in 2017, then learned through years of making, questioning and getting better. Today, I design at Rise across six marketing teams.

I like the whole job: finding the idea, making the case for it, giving it form, and making sure it survives the campaign, the handoff and the printer. The team should understand why the work works, then be able to keep using it well.

I want to make thoughtful, useful and unmistakable work from Lagos.
