About

Comic book buff, dinosaur wrangler, future daddy.

My earliest memory of design has to be the first time I came in contact with a games console. Ever since then, I’ve been fascinated by how design evokes emotion and drives people to act — and by the “superheroes” who build the products and systems that make daily life easier and more pleasurable.

If I can describe myself with one word, it would be inquisitive. Colleagues and friends say so because I genuinely question the reasons behind every decision and idea — to fully understand the thinking, propose better fixes where needed, and catch the loopholes that quietly devolve into scope creep.

Outside the work, I’ll happily talk your ear off about my love for snakes and my weird ability to taste water.

My biggest professional goal is to help create a standard for what brand and graphic design should be for African brands — raising the benchmark stakeholders use to measure brand success.
