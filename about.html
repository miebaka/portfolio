About

I ask better questions. Then I make the answer visible.

My earliest memory of design is a games console: the moment I first understood that form and function could conspire to make something irresistible. That question hasn’t left me. Seven years later, I’m in-house at Rise, shipping creative for more than 800,000 users across six sub-teams, and I’m ready to bring that to a team full-time. I’m also building an AI-augmented tool that uses open-source eye-tracking data to score whether an ad is making people look where it actually counts.

If I can describe myself with one word, it’s inquisitive. Colleagues say so because I question the reason behind every decision, not to be difficult, but because the loopholes you miss in the brief are the problems you end up solving after the brand is live. I look for the idea underneath before I look for the form.

Outside the work, I’ll happily talk your ear off about snakes and my strange ability to taste the difference in water. Still haven’t found a satisfying explanation for either.

Africa has some of the most compelling brands in the world. Most of them don’t look like it yet. I’m building a standard, not by borrowing from what works in New York or London, but by proving what’s possible from Lagos.
