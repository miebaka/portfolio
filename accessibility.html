Access is part of the work.

This portfolio aims to be usable with a keyboard, assistive technology and reduced motion preferences.

The standard I work toward

My target is WCAG 2.2 Level AA. This is an aim, not a claim of formal certification or complete conformance.

What is supported

Pages use semantic headings and landmarks, visible keyboard focus, skip navigation, native links and labelled forms. Visitors can reduce animation, and important content should remain accessible without JavaScript.

Known limitations

The interactive experiments gallery, project folders and footer signature rely on browser features and JavaScript for their enhanced behaviour. Core work and artwork are also offered through ordinary links and static alternatives. Accessibility varies across devices and assistive technologies.

Report a barrier

If a page is difficult to use, email a description, including the page, device, browser and assistive technology if relevant. You can also use the contact page.
