Reviewed and revised 31 October 2026. Applies to `writing/designing-for-disability-in-nigeria.html`. Original publication remains 29 September 2023; `dateModified` and a visible review note record this revision. No current figure is presented as though it were verified in 2023. This is source/editorial verification, not medical advice or a population survey.

## Limits and validation

No new national prevalence survey, medical guidance or estimate of 2026 prevalence is claimed. Global and country figures cannot be compared casually across definitions or ages. The article’s personal recollections remain the author’s account. Full external asset licensing and the entire recommended reading list were not independently authenticated; existing credits were retained. The original WHO-attributed African ranking could not be tied to an identified primary table and was removed, rather than labelled false with unsupported certainty.

Verification used owning sources and participant-authored institutional history, with web search and full-text retrieval; Firecrawl retrieved the World Bank’s large plain-text edition when the PDF exceeded the browsing limit. No external form was submitted. Only this review and the article were edited by this worker. No commit, push or deployment was performed.
