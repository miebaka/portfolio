## Removed rather than guessed

- Exact share-price peaks, April 2026 price, annual returns, market-cap endpoints, the repeated $180bn loss and the “four consecutive years” framing. A consistent primary historical pricing/market-cap record was not established in this pass. No substitute current quote was used.
- Wieden+Kennedy losing the bulk of its Nike engagement, a department-by-department creative exodus, blanket creative/R&D cuts, the retailer-account list and a purported 2022 reversal. The available primary sources did not substantiate these exact claims.
- Jordan's long “Failure” quotation, its championship-count setup and the exact 1997 attribution. No owning transcript was established here; the section now uses the verified Dream Crazy campaign without fabricating a Jordan source.
- Sneaker Freaker quotation, exact outlet discounts, queues/restock anecdotes, pandemic-demand causality, and Supreme/Hermès/Apple motive claims. These were not needed to make the bounded design argument.
- June 2024 lawsuit framing. Allegations are not findings; removing this unsupported detour avoids an unverified legal assertion.
- The unidentified purported 2021 campaign image and its year-wide negative comparison. Its source/date were not established. The removed image is not asserted to be fake. The remaining outlet image is described without an unverified discount claim.
- Unverified Hill/Fox Business quotations, $5bn 2026 marketing commitment, “Nike Mind headgear,” broad running-category victories, and unsupported New Balance/ASICS collaboration/market claims.

## Argument and publication safeguards

The designer's concern about emotional connection, desirability, product renewal and channel experience remains. Causal certainty has been replaced with an explicit authorial reading. “Defunding the Creative Engine” becomes “Losing the Thread of the Brand” because rising aggregate marketing expense contradicts a simple spending-cut thesis. Marketing expenditure is not treated as a measure of creative quality. Competitor growth is not treated as proof of causation.

The title, original publication date, six H2 section headings, article landmarks, image loading behavior and shared navigation hooks remain. No empty source placeholders or new unverifiable commercial promises were introduced. Metadata descriptions were updated to match the bounded argument.

## Verification limits

Primary releases, filings and agency records verify their stated figures/announcements, not the truth of every management interpretation. Market-cap/stock assertions and campaign provenance that could not be established were withdrawn. Image licensing/originality was not independently cleared. No empirical estimate of creative contribution to financial performance is claimed. Some source documents distinguish reported and currency-neutral results; the retained article names those bases where used. No contact messages, trades, publication or deployment were performed by this subtask.
