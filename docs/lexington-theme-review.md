## Jacobsen measurements and application

- Typography: desktop headings use roughly 18–24px, normal weight and tight tracking; the About heading reaches 30px at 500. Body text is 16px and UI text 14px, with approximately 28–32px heading line-height.
- Spacing: the desktop wrapper tops out at 1280px with 24px side padding, moving to 1360px with 48px padding at 2xl. The hero starts 128px from the top. Sections use 96px vertical padding, 48px row gaps and 32px column gaps.
- Grid: twelve columns with 32px gutters. Hero copy spans five columns and the image seven. The About content spans eight columns.
- Overlay: the hero pins while the next opaque section advances over it. Image reveals are progressive enhancement, with a reduced-motion path.

## What was implemented now

- Increased the homepage section rhythm to 128–192px and the internal rhythm to 80–128px.
- Reduced the home gutter toward Jacobsen's 24–48px range while retaining the portfolio's fluid grid.
- Reduced the About body size and widened its leading to make the section quieter and more readable.
- Kept one homepage-only shader beneath the pinned hero and removed the duplicated shader behind the scrolling content sheet.
- Removed the cursor-reactive canvas from inner pages.
- Restored native browser cursors for default, links/buttons and text entry.

The remaining ideas are a backlog, not a mandate. Proof rows, archive modes, sticky case-study facts and cross-linked capabilities are the strongest next candidates because they reinforce the existing evidence-first direction without changing the brand language.
