# KLT copy template

Use Know, Like and Trust as a page-level persuasion sequence, not three labelled blocks.

1. **Know** — Name the visitor's problem, Miebaka's role and the decision the page helps them make.
2. **Like** — Add one candid point of view or human detail in an inquisitive, decisive and warm voice.
3. **Trust** — Put a named project, role, process choice or verified result beside the claim it supports. Never invent an outcome or testimonial.
4. **Act** — End with one contextual verb-object action: “View the work”, “See how I work”, or “Start a conversation”.

## Five-second test

A visitor should be able to answer: Who is this? What does he do? Why him? What proves it? What should I do next?

## Voice rules

Use first person, British spelling, short declarative headings and one thought per sentence. Prefer 18–32 words per body paragraph. Avoid agency filler and unproved superlatives. Preserve legal text and article arguments; their precision is their Trust layer.

Sources: [Bob Burg](https://burg.com/2010/04/all-things-being-equal/), [Stanford Web Credibility Guidelines](https://credibility.stanford.edu/guidelines/), [Association for Psychological Science on influence](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/observer/universal-principles-of-influence).
