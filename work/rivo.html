Rivo

A financial brand built on warmth, not cold blue.

What we did

Brand identity, Brand strategy, Guidelines

Industry

Fintech

How do you make a fintech brand feel human?

Rivo was entering one of the most crowded markets in tech — where every challenger looks the same: cold blues, generic gradients, and not a single point of view.

We were asked to build an identity that signalled trust without disappearing into the category — warm enough to feel human, sharp enough to feel capable.

The challenge

Fintech lives or dies on trust, yet the category has flattened into a sea of interchangeable blues. Rivo needed to feel different on sight while still reading as safe with people’s money — a hard balance between warmth and authority.

The identity

We led with a warm, considered green over the category blue — ownable on sight and quietly confident. A humanist logotype softened the edges so a money brand could feel approachable, not corporate.

Rather than a fixed logo, we built a flexible system: components that scale from an app icon to a billboard without ever breaking.

The outcome

Rivo launched with a system the team could run themselves, and a look that finally matched the product’s ambition.
