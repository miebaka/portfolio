TEDx RSU

A global stage, made unmistakably local.

What we did

Brand identity, Creative direction, Print

Industry

Event

How do you honour a global brand while making it your own?

TEDx carries a strict global identity, yet every event needs its own character. The brief was to build something that honoured the TEDx system while capturing the spirit of Rivers State University.

And it had to hold up everywhere — on stage, in print, and on a phone screen — without falling apart.

The approach

Work within the TEDx rules, not around them. The red and wordmark stay sacred; character comes from type, motion and a local visual motif — one system that scaled from a phone to a stage backdrop.

The craft

Print was treated as a first-class citizen — tactile, considered collateral that matched the on-stage experience across programme, signage and social.

The outcome

A cohesive event brand that felt premium and on-system, applied consistently across every touchpoint on the day.
