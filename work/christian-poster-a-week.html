A Christian Poster A Week

One poster a week — scripture as bold graphic design.

What we did

Graphic design, Art direction, Typography

Type

Self-directed series

Can scripture be designed with real craft?

Faith content online is visually tired — soft gradients, stock doves, predictable type. I wanted to prove a verse could be designed with the same edge as a culture brand.

So I set a rule: one poster, every week. A fixed format and a hard deadline that would force decisions and build a recognisable body of work.

The discipline

Every week I chose a verse, found its single emotional idea, and built a poster around it — concept, typography, image treatment and finish. The constraint was the point.

The range

One idea per poster, said loudly. Expressive, varied typography — condensed, serif, display — held together by a consistent grid, with real craft on every finish.

The outcome

A growing, recognisable body of work that doubles as my most reliable proof of range — and a habit that keeps my craft sharp.
