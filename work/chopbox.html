ChopBox

An identity as warm and crave-able as the food.

What we did

Brand identity, Brand strategy, Packaging

Industry

Food brand

How do you make a brand you’d order from twice?

Food brands live or die on appetite appeal. ChopBox needed to feel warm, generous and instantly crave-able — the kind of brand you trust with dinner.

And it had to stay flexible enough to run across packaging, app and social without ever losing its flavour.

The approach

I built the brand from strategy out, checking every decision against one question — does this make you hungry? Warmth over neon; a friendly, rounded logotype over the loud reds every food app defaults to.

The system

Built for packaging from day one — a system designed to survive the box, not just the screen, with a toasted, appetising palette that owns its category.

The outcome

An appetising, ownable identity with a system the team could extend across products and packaging.
